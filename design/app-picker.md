# App picker surface

Mode: Operate.

Target: `docs/index.html`, `docs/app.js`, `docs/styles.css`.

The user’s task is to identify familiar Windows ARM64 apps, select a preferred artifact for each, and copy or export those publisher download URLs. Recognition, clear selected state, honest architecture labels, and easy scanning take priority over decoration.

Preserve the catalog, its evidence and dates, selection hashes, download formats, local saved selections, starter packs, clipboard fallback, and aria2 checksums. Refinement must not imply that header checks prove CPU architecture or Authenticode validity, or that Armite installs apps itself.

Use real app artwork in the catalog and selected list. Load it from this repository; retain a non-letter generic SVG fallback for an asset error. Keep the existing branding and factual copy. The shorter intro should let the picker appear sooner.

Verify desktop, intermediate, and phone sizes; search, category filtering, selecting/removing apps, alternate artifacts, combined URL export, and share URL restoration. Verify images for all 68 catalog records, including hidden unavailable entries.
